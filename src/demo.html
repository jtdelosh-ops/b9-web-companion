Silent · enable voice to hear him

While rolling, he occasionally stops, faces you, waves for about four seconds, then carries on. Choose Meet B-9 to invite him in: he rolls to the center, faces you, waves hello, then roams left, right, up and down. Closing him sends him rolling off the nearest side; Show repeats his entrance and greeting. After each wave, he rolls for another 16–26 seconds. Drag him while roaming: he pauses for three seconds, then resumes at the new position. Park keeps him still. “Retract head” withdraws the stem into the fixed fluted collar, leaving the bulb resting above it. Holds vary from 1.5–6 seconds. Voice starts muted; “Test TV voice” explicitly enables it.
