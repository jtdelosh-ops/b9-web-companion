B-9 / COMPANION PORTABLE WEB KIT · REVISION 14

A SMALL ADDITION TO YOUR WEBSITE

A familiar face.
A little personality.

The Robot and his compact remote can sit alongside your existing website. Choose Meet B-9 in the lower left. He rolls to the center, turns toward you, waves hello, then starts exploring. The same button then opens his compact remote.

Quiet on arrival. He waits off-screen until invited, then roams left, right, up and down after his greeting. Sound starts muted. Drag him anywhere; he pauses for three seconds, then carries on. Close him and he rolls off-screen; Meet B-9 or Show robot repeats his entrance and greeting. The remote can be dragged too, and closes back to a small button.
